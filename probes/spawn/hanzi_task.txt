Ideas to evaluate (don't stop at the first): per-component positional variants (X.left, X.right, X.top, X.bottom, X.surround… chosen by contextual GSUB after the IDC, the IDC itself zero-width, GPOS pulling the second part back over the first). Nesting (⿱田⿰亻木) needs quarter-size variants or depth-limited chains.
